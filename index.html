Subsystems

Hull Structure

Composite deck • Planing bow

The main chassis is designed in Autodesk Fusion 360 with internal bulkheads to distribute aerodynamic pressure while keeping total weight to a minimum. A 28° planing deadrise at the bow prevents plowing during water transitions.

  • Dimensions: 850 × 520 × 260 mm
  • Structure: High-density XPS core with composite skin
  • Mass Allocation: 2.40 kg dry chassis weight
  • Air Distribution: Internal channels routing flow directly into skirt perimeter

Segmented Skirt

24 individual finger segments

An open-finger segmented skirt system retains static cushion pressure beneath the hull. Independent fingers flex individually over uneven terrain without deflating the adjacent air cushion, maintaining a 20mm hover height.

  • Material: 110 g/m² polyurethane-coated ripstop nylon
  • Segment Count: 24 individually replaceable finger modules
  • Plenum Pressure: ~215 Pa calculated equilibrium pressure
  • Ground Clearance: 20 mm nominal operating float

Dual Powertrain

Independent lift & thrust fans

Decoupled brushless electric drives allow the lift impeller to maintain constant plenum volume while the horizontal thrust fan provides proportional forward acceleration.

  • Lift Drive: Outrunner BLDC motor with multi-vane static pressure impeller
  • Thrust Drive: Ducted propeller delivering up to 18.5 N forward thrust
  • Power Source: 4S (14.8V) 4500 mAh LiPo battery pack
  • Speed Controllers: Dual 40A telemetry-capable brushless ESCs

Steering Linkage

Dual-vane vectoring rudders

Dual symmetrical aerodynamic rudders operate directly in the thrust propwash. A metal-gear digital servo with zero-backlash ball links drives the rudders across a ±35° range for immediate yaw authority.

  • Rudder Configuration: Twin balanced NACA 0012 profile airfoils
  • Deflection Range: ±35° proportional deflection
  • Actuation: High-torque digital servo with rigid M2 ball-joint linkages
  • Yaw Moment: ~4.0 N·m calculated turning torque
